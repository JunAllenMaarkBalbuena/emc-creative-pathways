## 3. The perf defect: measured, then fixed

A temporary headless harness timed `lab._end_stroke()` on a 512×512 RGBA8
layer, brush 20, spacing 0.3 (6 px), opacity 1.0:

| Scenario | Before (`c85f7f2`) | After (`dbc9355`) |
|---|---|---|
| Typical stroke (~6.4 kpx region) | 10.7 ms | 6.3 ms |
| Full-canvas serpentine sweep (~262 kpx region) | **166.7 ms** | **7.8 ms** |

Root cause: three O(region) per-pixel GDScript loops at once — the strip
assembly (×4-channel byte copy), `_capture_region` (per-byte row copy), and
`_restore_region` (`set_pixel` per pixel, also hit by fill/clear undo).

Fix (`dbc9355`) — all three become native `Image` operations:

- **Assembly**: each strip → `Image.create_from_data` (tight RGBA8) → blit into
  a zero-filled region-sized buffer. `blit_rect` clips at the destination
  bounds, so the in-canvas part of every strip lands at exactly the byte
  offset the old loop computed — layout verified byte-exact by the tests.
- **Capture**: `img.get_region(inside)` (bulk C++) blitted into a zero-filled
  buffer; off-canvas rows stay transparent.
- **Restore**: `create_from_data` + `blit_rect` raw copy (replaces `set_pixel`),
  also speeding up full-canvas fill/clear undo.

Residual ~5 ms on a typical stroke-end is the **per-stroke Lanczos layer
thumbnail** (`generate_thumbnail(48)` + `ImageTexture.create_from_image` in
`main._end_stroke`) — a separate, pre-existing cost center, deliberately not
changed here (documented in §6).
